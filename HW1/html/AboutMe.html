About Me ✋

Hello! My name is Katherine Tamayo, and welcome to my crochet page. I'm currently a student at UMass Lowell, pursuing a degree in Computer Science. In addition to my studies, I have a deep passion for crafting, particularly crochet. I have started crocheting a few years ago, and it has quickly become one of my favorite hobbies. I'm still learning, but I enjoy completing projects by following tutotials and patterns. Crochet allows me to express my creativity and create beautiful, handmade items that I can share with friends and family.

On this page, you will find information about the basics of crochet, as well as some of my favorite projects that I have worked on. Whether you are a beginner looking to learn more about crochet or an experienced crocheter looking for inspiration, I hope you find something useful and enjoyable here.

Feel free to explore the links above to learn more about crochet basics and see some of my projects. If you have any questions or would like to share your own crochet experiences, please don't hesitate to reach out!

I hope you find it helpful and inspiring to start your own!
